Founders' House Kolkata: National-Level 72-Hour Residential Startup Hackathon

18 to 20 December 2026 · AIC Techno (Atal Incubation Centre), OFFBeat CCU, 36F Topsia Road, Kolkata, West Bengal 700039

Founders' House is a premier national-level 72-hour residential startup hackathon where 10 elite student founder squads selected from across India build production MVPs, validate with real users, and pitch live to top investors and mentors. Free to apply. Open to students pan-India. Teams of 3 to 5 members.

Organised by Hult Prize at Techno India University, in collaboration with AIC Techno (supported by Atal Innovation Mission, NITI Aayog).

Mission Tracks

  • Social Innovation & Social Business — Circular economy, water security, healthcare accessibility, rural supply chains, and green urban mobility.
  • Emerging Tech: AI, CS & IT, Cyber & EdTech — Agentic workflows, voice synthesis, decentralized security, and next-gen developer tooling.
  • Media, Entertainment & Creative Design — Spatial audio, generative storytelling, creator monetization, gaming universes, and Indic content.
