About the Program

Where the next generation of founders build the startups of tomorrow.

Founders' House is a 3-day residential startup sprint for India's most driven student teams. The top 10 selected teams live on-site at AIC Techno, Kolkata — receiving a real-world problem statement from experienced mentors, then validating, building, and pitching their solutions to a jury of investors.

This isn't a weekend project fair. It's an intense, immersive experience designed to compress months of startup learning into 72 hours.

The Journey

How It Works

Four steps from application to launchpad.

1

Apply as a Team

Fill out the application form and submit a 60–90 second selfie-style video pitch with your team of 3–5 members.

2

Screening

Applications are reviewed by the core team and mentors. The top 10 teams are shortlisted for the residential sprint.

3

The 3-Day Sprint

Live on-site for an intense 72-hour experience of building, validating, and iterating your startup idea.

4

Demo Day & Beyond

Pitch to investors and a jury, compete for awards, and unlock incubation and funding pathways.

The Experience

The 3 Days

72 hours of building, validating, pitching — and a little bit of Bollywood.

Day 1: Inspire & Form

Arrive, connect, and ignite the spark. Today is about absorbing the challenge and assembling your battle plan.

🏁

Check-In & Opening Ceremony

Welcome to your new home for the next 72 hours. Settle in, meet the other teams.

🎤

Founder Talks

Hear from real founders who've been where you are — raw stories, real lessons.

👋

Team Introductions

Quick-fire intros. Who are you, what drives you, and what are you building?

🧩

Problem Reveal

Mentors present the real-world problem statements. Your sprint begins now.

💡

Sprint Kickoff & Ideation

Brainstorm, sketch, and map out your initial solution approach.

🎯

Customer Discovery Games

Interactive exercises to sharpen your understanding of the user you're solving for.

Day 2: Validate & Build

The marathon day. Validate your assumptions, build your MVP, and prepare to pitch.

📋

Business Model Canvas Race

Map your entire business model against the clock. Speed meets strategy.

🔨

MVP Build Sprints

Heads down, hands on. Build the minimum viable version of your solution.

🧑‍🏫

Mentor Office Hours

1:1 sessions with mentors to sharpen your strategy, product, and pitch.

📊

Deck Building

Craft a compelling pitch deck that tells your startup's story in slides.

🎬

Bollywood Pitch Masala

Pitch your startup as a 60-second Bollywood movie trailer. Drama welcome. 🍿

👔

One Minute CEO

Explain your entire startup in 60 seconds. No slides, no props — just you.

📈

GTM Game Plan

Map your go-to-market strategy — who are your first 100 customers?

☕

Ghat-side Adda Pitch

End the marathon night with raw, informal pitches over bharer cha. Honest feedback from mentors, no corporate filters.

Day 3: Demo Day & Angel Adda

This is it. Refine, present, and make your case to the people who can write your first cheque.

💬

Feedback Frenzy

Rapid-fire feedback rounds to polish every edge of your pitch and product.

🔐

Escape Negotiation

A gamified challenge to sharpen your deal-making and negotiation skills.

🎭

Mock Pitches

Rehearse with a tough panel before the real deal. Last chance to iterate.

⚡

Investor Speed Dating

5-minute conversations with multiple investors. Make every second count.

🦈

The Great Angel Adda

The Kolkata edition of Shark Tank. Pitch to top angel syndicates & VC partners — real term sheets on the table.

🏆

Final Presentations

Your moment. Present to the full jury — investors, founders, and industry leaders.

🤝

Investor Row

Direct conversations with investors for incubation offers and funding talks.

🎬

The Josh Awards

The grand finale. Filmfare-style awards for the best teams across categories.
