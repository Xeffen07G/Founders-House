Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hult Prize at Techno India University ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Founders' House website and apply for the event.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, college/university name, team member details when you apply through the registration form.
  • Video Submissions: The selfie-style video pitch you submit as part of your application.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics data such as page views, browser type, and device information through Google Analytics.
  • Contact Form Data: Name, email, and message content when you use our contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and review your application for Founders' House
  • To communicate with you regarding your application status
  • To send event-related updates and logistics information
  • To improve our website and future events through aggregated analytics
  • To respond to your inquiries via the contact form

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • AIC Techno: Our collaboration partner for event logistics
  • Mentors and Jury: Application details shared for screening and evaluation purposes only
  • Service Providers: Google (Analytics, Forms) for website and application processing

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies through Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
